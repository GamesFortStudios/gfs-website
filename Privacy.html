PayNod // How we handle your data

PayNod Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

PayNod is an Android app by Games Fort Studios ("we", "us", "our") for recording salaries and advances between an employer and their staff. This page explains what PayNod collects, how it is used, and how to delete your account and data. It applies to PayNod in addition to our general Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this page applies to PayNod.

01Delete Your PayNod Account & Data

You can ask us to delete your PayNod account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  • Email gamesfortstudiosofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PayNod account".
  • Include the mobile number you use to sign in to PayNod.
  • We may contact that number to confirm the request is from you.
  • We delete the data within 30 days of confirming the request.

You can also start this from inside the app: open PayNod and choose Delete account.

What is deleted

  • For everyone — your login, your name, your mobile number and your notification token.
  • If you are an employer — the groups you created, the people you added to them, every salary, advance and repayment entry in those groups, and all attached payment screenshots.
  • If you are a staff member — your login and profile.

What is kept

  • Entries that an employer recorded about payments to a staff member stay in that employer's account, because they are also the employer's own records. They are deleted when the employer removes them or deletes their own account.

02Information We Collect

PayNod collects only what it needs to work.

  • Mobile number — to sign you in with a one-time code (OTP) and to link a staff member to the account their employer created for them.
  • Name — your name, and the names and job roles of the people an employer adds to a group.
  • Salary records — monthly salary amounts and the entries users record: salary paid, advance given and repayment, with the amount, date, payment method (cash, UPI or bank) and an optional reference number.
  • Payment screenshots — only when a user chooses to attach one to an entry as proof of payment. PayNod opens the system file picker for that one image and does not read the rest of your gallery.
  • Notification token — an identifier issued to your device so that the app can notify you about entries.

PayNod does not collect your location, your contacts list, your messages, or advertising identifiers, and it contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

03How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show each person their own salary account and balances.
  • To send an entry to the other person so they can accept or reject it.
  • To notify you when an entry needs your answer or has been answered.
  • To respond when you contact us for support or ask for deletion.

We do not use PayNod data for advertising or marketing, and we do not build profiles from it.

04Who Can See Your Records

  • An employer can see the groups they created and the records of the people in those groups.
  • A staff member can see only their own account. They cannot see other staff or other groups.
  • A payment screenshot can be seen only by the two people on that entry.

When an employer adds you by your mobile number, your name, number, role and salary are stored in their group before you sign in. If you believe you were added by mistake, contact us and we will help.

05Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.

  • Google Firebase — PayNod uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging (services of Google) to sign users in, store the records and deliver notifications. Google processes this data on our behalf. To send the one-time code and to check that a sign-in request comes from a genuine device, Google also processes your mobile number and basic device information.
  • Legal & safety — we may disclose information when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

06Storage & Security

PayNod data is stored on Google Cloud servers and is encrypted while it is sent between the app and the servers. Access rules on the server allow a record to be read only by the employer who owns it and the staff member it belongs to. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07Data Retention

We keep your account and records for as long as your account exists, so that both sides keep a full history. Data is deleted when you delete it in the app (for example an employer removing a person) or when you ask us to delete your account as described in section 01.

08Payments & Money

PayNod is a record book. It does not send, receive or hold money, and it never asks for your card details, UPI PIN or bank password. Payments are made outside the app, in the way you already pay, and are only recorded in PayNod.

09Children's Privacy

PayNod is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at gamesfortstudiosofficial@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can change your records in the app, and you can exercise any of these rights by contacting us at gamesfortstudiosofficial@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the app.
